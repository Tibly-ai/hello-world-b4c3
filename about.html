// about us

Every good program starts with a first line.

Ours was print("Hello, World") — and it never stopped feeling right. So we built a business around that same idea: start simple, say what you mean, and stop when you're done.

Why we exist

We got tired of watching small businesses pay for six-page websites nobody ever finished — bloated menus, stock photo carousels, forms that quietly went nowhere. Most owners just need one honest page: who you are, what you offer, and how to reach you. So that's what we build. Nothing you don't need, nothing that breaks.

Our philosophy

Simple isn't the same as small. A single well-written page can hold your whole story if you're clear about it. We treat every word and every button like it has to earn its place — the same way a good line of code does. Clarity first. Cleverness only if it helps.

A small manifesto

  • One page can be enough — if it's the right page.
  • Fast beats fancy. Every time.
  • If a visitor can't find your phone number in ten seconds, we failed.
  • Websites should ship like software: tested, working, live.
